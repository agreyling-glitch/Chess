A few steps to connect

From desktop
to chessboard.

Read the setup guide →

  1. Download & start

    Get the Windows x64 ZIP, extract it into a permanent folder, and open Companion. Wait for its status to say Ready.

  2. Pair with Ironwood

    In Companion, choose Show token. In Ironwood, open Help → Companion connections… and use that token to connect.

  3. Choose how you use it

    Select your Companion as an archive, an engine provider, or both. Your board stays in the browser.
