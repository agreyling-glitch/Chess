01 / ENGINES

Your choice of UCI engine.

Use any UCI-compatible engine through Companion for practice games and analysis. Adaptive Training vs AI currently supports Stockfish, Lc0, and Maia3. Install a supported package from its publisher or register an engine you already have. Returning to a discovered engine skips the discovery probe.

Turn collections into study material.

A few steps to connect

From desktop
to chessboard.

Read the setup guide →

  1. Download & start

    Get the Windows x64 ZIP, extract it into a permanent folder, and open Companion. Wait for its status to say Ready.

  2. Pair with Ironwood

    In Companion, choose Show token. In Ironwood, open Help → Companion connections… and use that token to connect.

  3. Choose how you use it

    Select your Companion as an archive, an engine provider, or both. Your board stays in the browser.
