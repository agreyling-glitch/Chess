- Menu icons open their own sections on click. **Analysis** opens the current game directly, and **Game Library** opens Saved Games. **Save current game** and **Import games** have dedicated icons. The active mode stays gold, and the browser tab title follows it. [Getting started](/help/getting-started/)
- **New game against Engine** and **Self-play** dock beside the Play menu on wide screens. Saved Games, Import Games, Lichess lobby, Scoresheet Entry, Elo Calculator, and Position Editor open as full-height panels beside the main menu bar.
- Play shows **Moves** and **Prediction**. Analysis enables move navigation and shows the tabs you’ve enabled. Results tabs appear when analysis results are available.
- An active game prompts you to **Return to game** or **Resign and switch modes**. Online games wait for server confirmation. Mode changes play a short tone when sound is enabled.
- Searchable Settings brings Companion Connections, storage information, local data, and Backup and Restore into one panel. Browser restore review and Companion backup-folder editing happen inline. [Saved games and backups](/help/saved-games-and-backups/)
- **Moves & sound** adds sound choices, a volume control, and a preview. Board tap also has octave and decay controls. **Analysis display** can place the balance bar on the left, on the right, or horizontally at the top of Analysis.
