- Added collection filtering, creation, renaming, deletion and membership actions to Saved Games for Browser and Companion storage. Deleting a collection preserves its games.
- Added destination collection choices to copies and moves in both directions. Browser backups now preserve collections and memberships, while Companion snapshots retain their existing full database coverage.
- Removed collection management from the old Companion archive window.
- Companion 0.1.7 adds indexed collection filtering and count-cache invalidation. It is included in the [Companion 0.1.7 public Windows release](/changelog/2026-10-30-companion-0-1-7/).
