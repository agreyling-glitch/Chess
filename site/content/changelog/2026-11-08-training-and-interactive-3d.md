- **Train against Engine** opens a full-height panel beside the Training menu on wide screens. Start a game or review progress in **Train**, and create or manage training profiles in **Profiles**. [Training help](/help/training-vs-ai/)
- **3D ↗** opens a separate board you can play on. Click or drag pieces and choose promotions there while the main window stays open. Both boards stay in sync and follow the same move rules. [Board controls](/help/board-and-view/)
- Switching between **Puzzles** and **Annotations**, or among **Drawings**, **Notes**, and **Chess Symbols**, keeps the divider and live board in place. [Puzzle help](/help/puzzles/)
