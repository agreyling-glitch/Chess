## Added

- A 3D board inside the main egui game workspace, with a fixed perspective, zoom, textured pieces, a marble board, lighting, and soft contact shadows.
- A **View** menu choice and a 2D/3D button beside Flip Board for switching board modes.
- A single 3D appearance control for brightness, contrast, and gloss, defaulting to 85.
- Coordinates, move highlights, and best-move arrows on the 3D board.
- Captured-piece icons and material-advantage information beneath each player's name, updated for the selected position during game review.
- Graphical piece choices in the promotion window.
- Marble, Wood, and Glass 3D themes in **View > Board > 3D theme**.
- A position preview beside the analysis details below the full-width expanded graph. It follows the selected or hovered position and marks the last move.
- An **Elo calculator** under **Tools** with rating controls, expected score, win/draw/loss projections, and an adjustable K-factor. Chess.com and Lichess modes are labeled as estimates.
- A searchable, topic-filtered **Help center** accessible from **Help > Contents** in the game and from the public website. Its Markdown articles have direct URLs that search engines can index, and new articles can be published without rebuilding the game WASM.
- Twenty plain-language help guides covering analysis scores, CPL, accuracy, move symbols, engine lines, FEN, PGN, Elo, the Position Editor, and importing puzzle positions from another site using a FEN or screenshot.

## Improved

- The board grows with the available workspace, with the 3D view positioned to use more of the screen.
- 3D rendering, move navigation, and zoom responsiveness have been refined.
- **Copy Board to Clipboard** in Game Moves now creates a board image from the active 2D or 3D view.
- Added desktop and WebGPU compatibility guidance to the Features page.
- Refined piece placement, square highlights, perspective, lighting, materials, antialiasing, and coordinate placement across the 3D themes.
- Desaturated the Wood theme's board to a near-neutral gray while retaining its grain and square contrast, and darkened its black pieces to distinguish them from the white pieces.
- Left-aligned and widened the Help contents sidebar for easier scanning.
- Expanded the gold menu border treatment across the application.
